🎯 Ad Creative Fatigue TrackerAn end-to-end data pipeline and automated diagnostic engine designed to identify ad creative fatigue across Meta Ads and Google Ads before campaign performance degrades.📌 Problem & Business ValueDigital marketing campaigns naturally experience creative fatigue when audiences see the same ad creatives too many times.
